What can you fix yourself?

Three clear categories, based on the tools, skills and risks involved.

Beginner-Friendly

Wiper blades, accessible air filters, some light bulbs, checking tire pressure and fluids. Tire rotation requires proper lifting equipment and wheel torque procedures.

Intermediate

12V battery replacement, oil changes, some spark plugs, brake pads, belts and accessible sensors. These need tools, vehicle-specific instructions and extra precautions.

Leave to a Shop

Transmission work, alignment, major suspension repairs, AC refrigerant service, brake lines, airbags and EV high-voltage components.
